# SURTR PCB review

Both boards use four copper layers and single-sided component assembly. On the head, the front RF routes reference L2 and the back X output references L3. The layer plots show both inner ground planes. The back route removes the amplified X output from the transformer input area. Two ground vias flank its signal via at 1.2 mm center-to-center. The plated SMA signal hole terminates that route without a separate signal via. In the geometry check, the minimum same-layer gaps from the head coax output traces to input traces are about 10.1 mm for X and 8.5 mm for Y.

The uniform head output traces are 0.34 mm wide. A two-dimensional electrostatic cross-section with 40 µm copper, a 0.50 mm side clearance, and assumed dielectric constant 4.2 predicts approximately 50 Ω using 0.2104 mm outer dielectric. The thinner 0.1164 mm stackup would give substantially lower impedance at the same width. Request the 0.21 mm outer dielectric geometry and have the fabricator confirm the finished width and impedance. The dipole arms and filter interconnects have circuit-defined impedances.

On the bias tee, the short antenna-side center/shield pairs cross intentionally isolated ground areas at the common-mode chokes. The single-ended uniform-line model applies to the plane-backed sections only. The choke transitions, pad discontinuities, SMA launches, and the X-layer transition remain outside the cross-section model. A VNA measurement of the fabricated boards should cover output match and cross-channel isolation, followed by a powered stability test with realistic antenna and cable terminations. The package-envelope 3D views show placement and connector direction. The silkscreen and placement sheets provide exact designators; the envelope views do not verify part polarity.
